{%- comment -%} MathJax 3. kramdown (math_engine: mathjax in _config.yml) turns $$...$$ into \(...\) inline and \[...\] on its own line, which MathJax renders by default. A single-dollar $...$ passes through kramdown untouched, so it is registered here as an inline delimiter too. Write \$ for a literal dollar sign. {%- endcomment -%}
