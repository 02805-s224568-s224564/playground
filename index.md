Every week we take one question about the Marvel universe as it is written down
on Wikipedia — who appears with whom, which characters hold the network
together, how the communities split — and answer it with network analysis
rather than with intuition. Each post shows the result, the reasoning behind it,
and links to the notebook that produced every figure.
